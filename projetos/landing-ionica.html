iônica is a Learning Management System (LMS) created by FTD Educação, one of the 50 most relevant publishing companies in the world. It allows students to access digital books and complete activities, teachers to create assignments and teach online classes, and school administrators to better manage their institutions.

02 · Landing page

Apresentar o aplicativo e convidar para o downloadIntroducing the app and inviting users to download it

No fim de 2023, o aplicativo mobile da iônica foi lançado para iOS e Android, e eu fiquei responsável pelo desenho da landing page. O objetivo era destacar as principais funcionalidades do app e criar um convite claro para que as pessoas fizessem o download.

In late 2023, iônica's mobile app launched for iOS and Android, and I was responsible for designing its landing page. The goal was to highlight the app's main features and create a clear invitation for people to download it.

Nesse primeiro momento, o aplicativo era focado nas necessidades dos estudantes, incluindo acesso a livros digitais e avisos enviados pela coordenação. A página precisava apresentar esse recorte de forma direta, visual e coerente com a identidade da iônica.

At that stage, the app focused on student-specific needs, including access to digital books and announcements from course coordinators. The page needed to present that scope in a direct, visual way that remained consistent with iônica's identity.
